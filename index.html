PRIVACY INTRODUCTION                                                                                                 
In 2021, Didi was exposed to have serious issues regarding user privacy leakage. It illegally collected a vast amount of user information, such as screenshots from mobile phone albums, clipboard and app list information, face recognition information, and more. Meanwhile, there were increased suspected incidents of improper use and leakage risks of information. For instance, users received harassing phone calls from Didi's financial department. There were also cases of account theft and large non-personal orders in different locations. Users believed that there was a risk of privacy leakage. These actions violated multiple laws and regulations. In July 2022, the Cyberspace Administration of China imposed a fine of 8.026 billion yuan on Didi Global Inc. and a fine of 1 million yuan each on Cheng Wei, chairman and CEO of Didi Global Inc., and Liu Qing.
